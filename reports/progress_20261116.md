Local has 25,663 word edits and 77,443 character edits; hosted has 27,204 word edits and 81,657 character edits. API-minus-local differences are **5.46 percentage points WER** and **3.79 points CER**. These are error rates, not accuracy percentages. The configured local system has lower observed corpus errors on this frozen pool; that does not establish statistical significance, population-wide superiority or a causal effect of hosting.

Both systems have substantial errors against the published silver references. The earlier five-clip validation pilot favored the API, which did not establish a test-set ranking.
