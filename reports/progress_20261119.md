## Method and results

The November 12 frozen protocol specifies 2,000 paired source-video resamples within each channel, seed 42, with 2.5th/97.5th percentiles. Each draw samples three video selections from Channel A and nineteen from Channel B, with replacement. The same video multiplicities apply to both systems, all their paired clips and every reporting subset. The groups are filtered after full-pool sampling. Corpus totals are summed before division, preserving reference-length weighting. Groups below ten represented videos have no interval; no zero-denominator draw was discarded or replaced. All displayed intervals use 2,000 valid draws.

Differences below are **local minus API**, in percentage points; negative means lower local error.

| Metric, all 1,198 clips | Observed difference | Exploratory 95% interval |
|---|---:|---:|
| Corpus WER | -5.46 pp | [-9.79, -2.31] pp |
| Corpus CER | -3.79 pp | [-8.74, -0.38] pp |

Both overall intervals are below zero under source-video resampling conditional on the two observed channels and fixed silver labels. This supports the local system's observed lower error in this pool. It does not isolate hosting as the cause, establish a confirmatory significance finding, measure provider rerun variability or generalize to all Nepali speakers.

The first three CMI bands have WER intervals below zero. The two higher-CMI bands have wide WER intervals spanning both directions, despite larger local-favoring point estimates. The highest band's gap is -13.87 pp, interval [-34.18, +5.24] pp. Separate band intervals are not simultaneous intervals or a formal test of differing CMI-degradation slopes. The previously measured CMI–utterance-WER correlations remain near zero; uncertainty here targets corpus system differences, not a CMI correlation confidence interval.

## Influence, source and reference checks

- Local WER is lower on 488 clips, tied on 372 and higher on 338. These equal-clip-weighted descriptions are distinct from the corpus gap; no clip-independence test is used.
- Removing any one source video leaves local lower in corpus WER and CER. WER gaps across all 22 removals range -6.28 to -4.38 pp; CER gaps range -4.89 to -2.58 pp. No single-video removal reverses the point-estimate direction.
- Removing either whole channel also preserves the point-estimate direction. Channel A itself has only three videos, so no within-channel interval is reported. Channel B's WER interval spans zero. These source checks are descriptive, not adjustment for confounding.
- Duration-group WER points favor local in all four groups. The longest quartile favors the API slightly on CER (+1.55 pp local-minus-API), so local is not lower in every descriptive metric/group.
- Same-50 published-reference WER gap: +6.16 pp, interval [-9.23, +24.77]. Corrected-reference gap: +6.23 pp, interval [-9.21, +24.75]. Their point direction favors the API, but both wide intervals span zero. This diagnostic audit does not establish an opposite population ranking. Its band intervals are all withheld because each has fewer than ten videos.
